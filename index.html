Key Healthcare Scenarios

Engineered for Clinical Precision

Addressing the three primary operational challenges in modern healthcare IT.

Scenario 1
📅

Efficient Appointment Booking

Patients log in, select their specialist, and submit booking requests in real time. Hosted on AWS EC2 with Flask handling high-concurrency requests seamlessly during peak clinic hours.

  • Dynamic real-time specialist matching
  • Zero scheduling conflicts or overlap
  • Sub-second database writes in DynamoDB
Scenario 2
🛡️

Secure IAM User Management

MedTrack utilizes AWS IAM roles and least-privilege policies. Patients manage their own bookings, while doctors access clinical records and diagnostic queues under strict compliance protocols.

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
  • Isolated doctor and patient permissions
  • Encrypted passwords with Werkzeug security
Scenario 3
📋

Medical History & Diagnosis

Doctors and patients enjoy instant access to comprehensive medical records. Doctors upload diagnosis reports that update visit statuses and immediately trigger AWS SNS notifications.

  • Lifelong medical timeline preservation
  • Instant diagnosis report submission
  • Multi-channel automated SNS alerts
